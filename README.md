# Olist E-Commerce Sales Analysis

*A Business Intelligence project analyzing the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset — turning raw transactional data into actionable business insights through SQL and visualization.*

**Stack:** PostgreSQL · Tableau

## Overview

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace connecting small retailers to major sales channels. This project analyzes data across 9 relational tables to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product category trends, seller performance, payment behavior, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction — surfacing insights to support business decision-making and operational improvement.

## Key Insights

- R$16M in total revenue, with São Paulo emerging as the top-performing region
- 96.88% of customers were one-time buyers — a strong signal of reach, but a clear opportunity to invest in retention
- Average Order Value (AOV) reached R$161
- Credit cards dominated the payment mix at 73.92%, reflecting strong digital payment adoption
- A small group of sellers contributed a disproportionate share of total revenue
- Sales were concentrated across a handful of high-performing product categories
- Roughly 92% of orders were delivered on time
- Delivery delays showed a measurable impact on customer review ratings

## Dataset

- **Source:** Kaggle — Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist
- **Period:** 2016–2018
- **Scale:** ~100,000 orders across 9 relational tables
